# Contributions

Identify the source and license of every contribution and confirm that you
have authority to submit it. Do not include credentials, private data, or
third-party material without its required permissions and notices.

Contributions expressly submitted under this project's custom
[noncommercial license](LICENSE) use those terms. Contributors retain their
copyright. Submission alone does not assign copyright or grant the project
owner commercial relicensing rights. Before incorporating an outside
contribution into a separately licensed commercial version, the maintainer
must obtain a separate express grant or otherwise verify that the required
rights already exist. Record that scope; do not assume it from a pull request.

Preserve attribution and provenance records, identify modified files and
dates, and update build instructions when changing dependencies. Explain
what changed and how it was verified. Do not present modified versions as
official releases.
